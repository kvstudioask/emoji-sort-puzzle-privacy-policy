Privacy Policy

Emoji Sort Puzzle by KVStudio
Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KVStudio (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the mobile game Emoji Sort Puzzle (the “App”). We designed the App to be lightweight and respectful of your privacy.

1. Who we are

The App is published by KVStudio. For privacy questions, contact us at kvstudio.games@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App and which services are enabled, we may process:

  • Gameplay progress stored on your device — such as unlocked levels, selected theme, settings, and similar preferences saved locally (for example via on-device storage).
  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction data — when ads are shown, advertising partners may collect device advertising IDs and standard ad measurement data.
  • Approximate technical data — such as device type, OS version, app version, language, and crash/diagnostic signals needed to keep the App stable.
  • Optional share actions — if you use share features, the content you choose to share is sent through the sharing app you select (for example a messaging app). We do not receive the contents of those private messages.

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or precise location to play the game.

3. How we use information

  • To run core gameplay and remember your progress and settings on your device.
  • To show advertisements that help keep the App free (including rewarded ads you choose to watch).
  • To measure basic performance, fix bugs, and improve the App.
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App may use Google AdMob (and related Google advertising services) to display ads. Google and its partners may collect and process data as described in Google’s policies, including the use of advertising identifiers. You can learn more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & privacy overview

On Android, you can reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings (for example: Google → Ads, or Privacy / Ads settings depending on your OS version).

5. Analytics

If analytics are enabled in a given build, we use them only to understand feature usage and stability (for example which screens are opened or whether a level fails to load). Analytics are not used to sell personal profiles.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with:

  • Service providers such as Google (ads / measurement) who process data on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own policies.
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect users, our rights, or safety.

7. Children

The App is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of selling data. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Where required by platform or regional rules, ads shown in the App may be treated as non-personalized or otherwise restricted.

8. Data retention

Local game progress remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the App. Advertising and measurement partners retain data according to their own retention schedules.

9. Your choices

  • Clear local progress anytime via your device’s App info → Storage → Clear data.
  • Opt out of personalized ads through your device advertising settings.
  • Uninstall the App to stop further local collection by the App.
  • Contact us to ask questions about this policy.

10. International users

Our advertising and infrastructure partners may process data in countries other than where you live. Where required, those transfers rely on appropriate safeguards provided by those partners.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
